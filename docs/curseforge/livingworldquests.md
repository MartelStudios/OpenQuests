# Living World Quests

📦 **Quests and village characters for Living World Events: every Blood Moon, drill and ambush gets a goal in your journal.**

[Living World Events](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/living-world-events) fills your world with Blood Moons, Scarak swarms around Sprocket's drills, bandit ambushes and runaway goblins. This pack gives each event a reason to go out and meet it: chains that walk you from a first blood shard to an awakened weapon, villagers with commissions and a secret of their own, and achievements for the rarest drops. Works in multiplayer and singleplayer, on new or existing worlds.

## ✨ What is inside

38 quests in English and French, across three chapters of a **Living World Events** group in the journal, and six village characters.

🖼️ **Every quest has its icon**, from a blood shard to the Crimson Scythe, and each chapter is drawn as a shape: the chronicles in rows, the legends as a crest, the achievements as a crescent moon.

📝 **Two texts per quest.** The HUD says what to do: *Collect 20 Blood Shards*. The journal says how to get there: *Hold a Blood Moon to its last stage and a crowned Tyrant comes for you.*

🎁 **Every quest pays something from its own event**, often what the next step asks for: moonstones after the blood shards, deep ore after the drill, mithril for the awakened weapons.

***

## 📜 Chronicles

Fifteen quests, handed to every player, that follow each event from its first encounter to its finest gear.

🩸 **The Blood Moon.** Twenty blood shards, a Blood Charm, a Blood Moon Omen, then a Moonfang Staff or a Bloodletter.

⛏️ **Sprocket's drill.** Scaraks around the drill, a Mini Drill for saving it, a Tinkerer's Flare to call him back, then a Brood Stinger.

🛡️ **Iron and moon.** The four pieces of Bloodforged Warlord armor.

🗡️ **The ambushes.** Eight bandits, then the brute who leads the charge.

💍 **Room for charms.** An Accessory Pouch, then a pendant, a bracer or a lamp to put in it.

🌱 **The saplings.** The Greenwood Pathfinder armor, then a Razorleaf Bow.

Each sub-quest of an armor or a weapon names its bench, its tier and its ingredients.

***

## 🏛️ Legends

Thirteen commissions from six characters. Each one takes on an ordinary villager, a single person shared by the whole server, and hands out its quests only when you talk to it.

* 📖 **Yselle, the Archivist**, keeps the ledger of the Blood Moons and writes nothing down without proof. One account in it has no name.
* 🔧 **Rivet, the village mechanic**, hears a drill in trouble before anyone else, and worries most when it goes quiet.
* 🧺 **Mado, the robbed merchant**, numbers every shelf, even the empty ones since a goblin ran off with his savings.
* 🪖 **Tavin, the road veteran**, still walks on the ditch side of every path.
* ⚒️ **Sena, the armorer**, wants to see where the weight of a full Warlord suit really goes.
* 🍃 **Brindle, the Kweebec scout**, measures paths in patches of shade and wants a tree where the saplings stop in the sun.

🔍 **Yselle's last three commissions are a small story of their own**, told across two characters. Her confession, what Tavin saw that night, and what she does about it.

🔁 **Each step follows at once.** Hand in what a character asked for and the next commission, or their thanks, opens in the same conversation.

## 🏆 Achievements

Ten achievements for the moments Living World Events makes rare: the crowned Tyrant's skull, the Crimson Herald in its final form, a Treasure Goblin caught, his whole sack, the Broodmother, Sprocket's own drill, the Crimson Scythe torn from the Herald, any awakened weapon, every sapling home and a Sapling Buddy that refuses to leave your side.

***

## 🛠️ Installing

Requires **Living World Events** 1.9.5 or newer, **OpenQuests** and **OpenQuests Core** 5.4.0 or newer, **OpenDialogues** 2.4.0 or newer and **OpenQuests Dialogues** 1.3.0 or newer. CurseForge installs them with it.

This pack carries no code of its own. By hand, drop the zip in `mods/` next to the jars of the mods above.

🌱 The sapling quests need the Kweebec Saplings event, which Living World Events ships turned off. Turn it on in its config to play them.
