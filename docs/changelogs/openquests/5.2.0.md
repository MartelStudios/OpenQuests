### New
- Team quests: one quest per party or faction, shared by every member, online or not. Progress counts for the whole team.
- A `Team` assignment scope and a `TeamTypes` asset, both offered in the asset editor. The `party` team type ships with the mod: every player gets a personal party they can invite friends into.
- Player commands: `/oquest team invite`, `accept`, `rename`, `leave` and `members`.
- Admin commands: `/oquest team admin create`, `join`, `leave` and `members`, which also work on offline players.
- A player who leaves a team keeps a copy of the quests the team was running.
- Team changes reach players connected to another server sharing the same storage.

### Fixes
- Rewards earned while offline are given once on reconnection, never twice.
- Rewards of a shared quest are given once per member.
