### New
- Quests remember who handed them out, and follow-up quests keep that giver.
- {giver} in a quest's texts shows the name of whoever handed it out, or "someone".
- A Text.<name> tag on a quest fills {name} in its title and descriptions.
- RequiresQuest can watch a list of quests whose counts add up (QuestAssetIds).

### Fixes
- Finished steps of a composite quest show their title with their quest's parameters.
