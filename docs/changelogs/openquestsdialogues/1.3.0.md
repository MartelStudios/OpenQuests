### New
- A quest handed out in a dialogue remembers the NPC who gave it, and shows its name.
- Deliver and Dialogue quests and NearCharacter constraints can aim at that NPC (GiverNpc) or any NPC of its character (GiverCharacter).
- RequiresQuest and QuestCooldown conditions can count only the quests given by the NPC being talked to (GivenBy), and RequiresQuest takes a list of quests.
- QuestCooldown condition: a delay after one of the player's quests ended.
- A quest aimed at an NPC fails once that NPC dies or loses its character, and its holders hear of a death.

### Requirements
- Requires OpenQuests 5.4 and OpenDialogues 2.4 or later.
