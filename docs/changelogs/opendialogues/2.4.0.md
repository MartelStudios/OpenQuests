### New
- Cooldown condition: a real-time delay after a dialogue was opened or answered, offline time included.
- OpenedAfter condition: a dialogue opened more recently than another, such as an offer made since the last thanks.
- Opened, Answered, Cooldown and OpenedAfter take With, to count only what was said with the NPC being talked to, or with its character.
- NearCharacter distribution filter: keeps characters apart, such as one farmer per village.
- An NPC that loses its character is forgotten from player histories, so the next NPC carrying it starts afresh.

### Fixes
- A new NPC knows the characters around it before it draws one.
- The NPCs of a closing world give their characters back.
