### New
- Every Kweebec village now has its own farmer, with a name of his own and a story with each player.
- Help him step by step: bring what he needs for a new hoe and a bucket, pick his ripe harvest, then sow his next crops.
- Once he trusts you, he keeps asking for a hand, and only offers a harvest when the crops are ripe around him.
- Each farmer remembers what you did for him, not for the farmer of the next village.

### Requirements
- Requires OpenQuests 5.4, OpenDialogues 2.4 and OpenQuests Dialogues 1.3 or later.
