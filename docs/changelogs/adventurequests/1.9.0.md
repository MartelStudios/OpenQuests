### New
- The Kweebec merchant's sap delivery now goes to Ginkel, their old master in the Forgotten Temple, who sends the essence of the sacred tree back to the village for a reward of 10 silver bars.

### Changes
- Bramwell, the Klops trader of the Forgotten Temple, is gone.

### Fixes
- The temple memories quest only counts the minute spent inside the temple: leaving pauses it.
- Forgotten Temple characters keep their names and stories when the temple instance closes.
- Finishing the intro now abandons the "skip the intro" quest.

### Requirements
- Requires OpenQuests 5.3 or later.
