### New
- Four new Kweebec village stories. A young weaver wants the silk spiders dragged off its loom and weaves you an armor piece of your choice from it. A guard wants the map Outlander scouts drew of the village. Another guard fell asleep on watch and needs the three spears Trorks stole back before the captain notices. A sapling who no longer dares gather reeds in the marsh asks you to clear out the snakes.
- Spiders, Outlanders and Trorks drop the stolen goods only while you are on the quest.
