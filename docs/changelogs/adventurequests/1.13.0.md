### New
- The Kweebec farmer now asks for a hand with more of his crops: carrots, cauliflowers, cotton, lettuces, onions, potatoes and wheat, whenever they are ripe around him.

### Changed
- His aubergine and pumpkin harvests now ask for one ripe crop instead of two.

### Requirements
- Requires OpenQuests 5.4, OpenDialogues 2.4 and OpenQuests Dialogues 1.3 or later.
