### New
- The Kweebec farmer's second tier grows: he may now ask you to sow carrots before the frost, or to bring him seeds after mice emptied his sack.

### Changed
- "Before the frost" and "Out of seeds" are now the farmer's quests: the worried farmers who gave them are gone, and the farmer of each village offers them instead.

### Requirements
- Requires OpenQuests 5.4, OpenDialogues 2.4 and OpenQuests Dialogues 1.3 or later.
