### New
- A robbed Kweebec merchant asks you to take back the three crates goblins stole from its cart. Goblins drop them only for this quest, and the merchant lets you pick your reward from the recovered goods.

### Changed
- The Feats chapter group is now called Achievements in English.

### Requirements
- Requires OpenQuests 5.5 and OpenDialogues 2.4.1 or later.
