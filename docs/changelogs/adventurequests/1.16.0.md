### New
- The Kweebec farmer opens his last tier, the great blight: once you have made him two tools, he needs you to save what the boars left of the harvest, or to chase the foxes away from his rabbits, for iron and life essence.

### Changed
- The ruined harvest and foxes at the burrows quests are now the farmer's: the villagers who gave them are gone.

### Requirements
- Requires OpenQuests 5.4, OpenDialogues 2.4 and OpenQuests Dialogues 1.3 or later.
