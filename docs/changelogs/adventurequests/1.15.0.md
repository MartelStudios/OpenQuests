### New
- The Kweebec farmer opens a third tier, tools: once you have sown for him twice, he asks you for dry hay bales, a new scarecrow or a watering can, and pays in copper and seeds of crops no village grows.

### Changed
- The soggy hay, scarecrow and watering can quests are now the farmer's: the villagers who gave them are gone.

### Requirements
- Requires OpenQuests 5.4, OpenDialogues 2.4 and OpenQuests Dialogues 1.3 or later.
