### New
- Five new Feran camp stories, bringing the camps to nine.
- The voice of the chief asks for sturdy chitin, then your oath as a friend of the camp, and pays in gold, Feran torches and a bone skull.
- Three hunts: a crocodile at the oasis, hyenas digging under the stores, vultures diving on the drying meat. A Feran only asks when the prey is within 150 blocks of the camp, so these stories are rare.
- A salt trader sends you to a Kweebec merchant friend in the forest with ten salt; once the salt arrives, the Kweebec sends four pumpkins back to the desert.
