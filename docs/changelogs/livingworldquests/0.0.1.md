### New
- 38 quests in English and French for Living World Events, across three chapters: Chronicles, Legends and Achievements.
- Chronicles follow the Blood Moon, Sprocket's drill, the ambushes and the saplings from the first encounter to the finest gear.
- Six village characters hand out thirteen commissions, including a three-part story told across two of them.
- Ten achievements for the rarest moments: the crowned Tyrant, the Crimson Herald, the Broodmother, a Treasure Goblin, Sprocket's own drill and more.
- Every quest has its icon and pays a reward from its own event.

### Requirements
- Requires Living World Events 1.9.5, OpenQuests 5.4, OpenDialogues 2.4 and OpenQuests Dialogues 1.3 or later.
