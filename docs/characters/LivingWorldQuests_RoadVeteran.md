# Tavin, Road Veteran

Identity `LivingWorldQuests_RoadVeteran`. One living carrier across the universe, with the `Feran_Civilian` group as its carrier pool. The distribution draws on NPCs outside instances and leaves existing interactions in place.

Tavin used to escort wagons, including Mado’s. He gives advice in short, practical sentences and wants drivers to be able to use the road again.

Sample line: “Look for the brute who calls the charge. Drop that one, and the next wagon has a chance.”
